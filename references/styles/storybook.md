# Style: picture storybook

The video looks like a physical children's picture book filmed lying flat on a wooden desk: a leather-bound cover with gold ornamental corners opens like a real book, then the camera holds a flat, head-on view of each cream-paper spread as it "reads itself" — a circular illustration on the left breathing with small looping motion, and serif body copy typing itself in on the right. The mood is warm, nostalgic, and gentle: soft pastel scene-paintings inside a locket-shaped frame, muted earth-tone data callouts, and a plush round mascot that waves through every scene. It reads as a bedtime-story explainer: cozy rather than corporate, tactile rather than flat-UI.

## Signature
| Item | On screen |
|---|---|
| Base surface | Dark wood-grain desk background (~#40291a–#4a3320) visible around the book at all times, with faint soft-focus dust/light motes drifting over it |
| Book object | Closed cover is deep maroon leather (~#461912 to #632c21) with a thin gold double-rule border (~#b2965e) and gold diamond corner ornaments; opened pages are matte cream paper (~#f4ead4 family) with a visible center gutter/spine shadow and a small triangular page-fold mark at the bottom center |
| Illustration frame | Every left page holds one circular "locket" window (thin brown ring) framing a single flat-vector scene; two small leaf sprigs pinch the top of the circle like a brooch |
| Palette per scene | Each chapter reskins the circle in one soft gradient: indigo night sky (~#2b2d5a) with cream stars, dawn sky fading to sun-glow (~#feeeb6) over green hills (~#9bc278), pale sky blue (~#a9d9f0) with white clouds and motion streaks, warm orange coin-shower sky (~#f8cf94) with gold coin icons, mint-green sky (~#c8e3d1/#dcecd7) with a picket fence and floating hearts, and a dusk gradient (purple ~#7a6485 to rose ~#aa5776 to warm hill ~#d9764f) for the closing scene |
| Mascot | A single round, blob-bodied chibi character (terracotta-orange body ~#af6648, blushed cheeks, simple dot eyes, one arm always raised waving) stands in every illustration, wearing a small nameplate/badge on its belly and topped with a small pulsing sparkle/star icon over its head |
| Typography | Cover title in a gold calligraphic/serif display face on maroon; page headings in a bold dark serif (near-black on cream); a smaller hand-brushed script line beneath each illustration acts as the chapter label; body copy is a plain reading serif; small gray caption line bottom-right credits a source |
| Data callouts | Key numbers/phrases are drawn in a warm terracotta-red accent (~#bc6040) with a hand-drawn underline stroke that draws itself in; comparisons appear as simple flat horizontal bar pairs (muted taupe ~#bda081 vs. accent terracotta ~#bc6040) and small rounded "old value → new value" tag chips with the old value struck through |
| Ornament | A thin horizontal rule with a small centered diamond mark separates every page heading from its body text; page numbers sit centered at the bottom of each page in the same brown ink |

## Direction
Build the video as a physical picture book lying flat on a warm wood-grain desk, shot straight-on with no camera movement except a brief perspective "lift" when the cover opens. Use a cozy, nostalgic palette: a leather cover (deep maroon or another rich, muted hue) with a thin gold double-line border and diamond corner ornaments, opening onto matte cream paper spreads with a visible center gutter. Give every left-hand page one circular "locket" illustration bordered by a thin ring and two small leaf sprigs, and reskin its background gradient per chapter to match that chapter's mood (e.g. night sky, sunrise, open sky, a themed motif) rendered in soft, flat, pastel vector shapes. Populate every scene with one small recurring plush/blob mascot — simple dot eyes, one arm perpetually raised in a wave, a tiny badge on its body, and a pulsing sparkle above its head — so it functions as the storyteller's constant companion across chapters. On the right-hand page, set a bold serif heading, a thin rule with a centered diamond divider, and body copy that reveals progressively (as if being typed or read aloud); pick one warm accent color (terracotta, coral, or similar) reserved only for key numbers/phrases, drawn with a hand-underline stroke, and reuse that same accent for any simple flat bar-chart comparisons or "old value → new value" tag chips. Add a small brushed-script chapter label under each illustration and centered page numbers at the bottom. Structure the narrative as a fixed sequence of short chapters (roughly 3–5 seconds each), transitioned by a physical page-turn/curl animation rather than a cut or crossfade, and close on a clearly marked "The End"-style page before fading to black. Score it with one continuous, softly rhythmic plucked/bell-like musical loop that fades in at the start and out at the end, and place one bright, full-spectrum chime/whoosh sound effect at every page-turn to punctuate the chapter changes.
