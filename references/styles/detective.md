# Style: detective evidence board

This clip reads like a detective's evidence corkboard being filmed and slowly revealed: a warm, lamp-lit cork bulletin board covered in pinned index cards and handwritten sticky notes, centered on a "mugshot" placeholder card for an unidentified suspect. Instead of cutting between shots, a single camera pans and tracks sideways across the board as new evidence cards appear one by one, each strung to the suspect card with taut red thread, building toward a wide "conspiracy board" pull-back. A camera-flash whiteout is the only hard cut in the video, swapping the mystery card for a clean "identified" result card stamped closed. The pacing feels like a true-crime documentary graphics package: steady ticking/pulsing music under a slow reveal, a stab of sharp hits, one big swell exactly on the flash-cut, then a fade to quiet.

## Signature
| Item | On screen |
|---|---|
| Backdrop | tight, textured cork-board surface, warm brown (observed ~#89552F, #694929, #917350), vignetting to near-black at the frame edges (~#17100A) as if lit by a single desk lamp |
| Suspect card | tall grey "mugshot" placeholder: black silhouette bust, a white "?" for a face, faint horizontal height-ruler ticks, and a solid black name-plate bar along the bottom |
| Evidence cards | cream/off-white lined index cards (~#F0E6CE–#EFE4CE), pinned slightly askew, each with a bold headline, a small numbered tag in the corner, and a tiny citation line along the bottom edge |
| Sticky notes | small handwriting-font notes in muted mustard yellow (~#A19347 as lit) and dusty pink/lavender (~#B78F9A as lit), scattered at random tilts around the board edges, carrying half-finished questions |
| Red accents | round red pushpins and taut red string joining them into a web (reds observed roughly #C8271D–#FE5E50), red hand-drawn circles/underlines marking a "found" value, a red strike-through-and-replace on a corrected number, and a red rubber-stamp outline near the end |
| Typography | a rounded sans-serif for headings and on-card numbers, paired with a casual marker/handwriting font for sticky notes and red hand-annotations; small, thin caption-style type for source lines |
| Camera | fixed distance/angle, only pans, tracks, and slowly zooms across the board — no shot-to-shot cutting except one flash transition |
| Reveal card | plain white card with a red small-caps eyebrow label, a large centered title, a black name-plate footer, and a red stamped badge, closing on one line of caption text at the very bottom of frame |

## Direction
Style an investigation-corkboard reveal video: a fixed camera looking at a warm, dimly lit cork bulletin board with heavy vignetting toward near-black edges, as if lit by a single desk lamp. Center a tall grey "unidentified subject" placeholder card — silhouette bust, big question mark, faint ruler ticks, black name-plate bar — pinned up with red pushpins. Reveal supporting evidence one card at a time by panning/tracking the camera across the board rather than cutting: cream, slightly tilted lined index cards, each with a bold headline, a small numbered evidence tag, and a thin citation line at the bottom. Build each headline with a fast letter-by-letter typewriter reveal, then animate its numbers in place — cross out and replace a value in red, sweep a dial needle while a percentage counts up, grow a bar in a bar chart and circle the winning one with a red hand-drawn ring. Scatter handwritten sticky notes in muted yellow and dusty pink around the board edges at random tilts, each holding a short half-finished question. About three-quarters through, pull the camera back to a wide shot where every evidence card is connected to the central card by taut red string in a spoke/web pattern, timed to the loudest point of the music. Make the one and only hard cut a full-frame camera-flash whiteout that swaps the placeholder card for a clean white result card: a red small-caps eyebrow label, a bold centered title, a black name-plate footer, and a red stamped badge, with one caption line typing in beneath it before the whole frame fades to black. Score it with a steady, evenly spaced ticking/pulsing rhythm through the build-up, a handful of sharp stabs just before the climax, one dominant swelling hit exactly on the flash-cut, and a smoothly decaying tail to silence afterward — no vocals. Keep the palette to warm cork browns, cream paper, near-black shadow, and a single red accent used for every pin, thread, hand-annotation, and stamp; pair a clean rounded sans-serif for structured text with a casual marker/handwriting font for anything meant to look hand-written.
