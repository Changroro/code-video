# Style: comic book issue (cover, page turns, splash pages)

A video that stages its content as a physical vintage comic book being read on camera: a portrait-oriented comic page/spread sits centered in the landscape frame against a near-black vignette background, and a virtual camera pans and pushes into individual panels the way a motion-comic or Ken Burns documentary reveals a print page. The book opens with a magazine-style cover, then turns through several two-page spreads of a 2x2 panel grid, and closes on a colophon-style back cover. Panels are built from flat pop-art color blocks (red, gold/orange, blue), Ben-Day halftone dot textures, and sunburst ray backgrounds, with comic sound-effect bursts and a speech-bubble mascot giving it an energetic, Silver-Age-superhero-comic / 1960s advertising-comic feel. The mood is upbeat, punchy, and declarative, reinforced by a continuously driving instrumental music bed with frequent hard transient hits.

## Signature
| Item | On screen |
|---|---|
| Base/ground | Near-black navy vignette (~#000010–#101020) fills the canvas outside the page; the comic page/spread itself sits on a cream/off-white paper color (~#f8f8f0, ~#f0e8d0) |
| Panel backgrounds | Flat color blocks per panel: warm red (~#e03028), golden-yellow/orange (~#f8d038, ~#f8b020), sky/cobalt blue (~#38b8e0, ~#1848a0, ~#3068c8), plus a dark maroon (~#581010, ~#902018) on the "next issue" teaser page |
| Texture | Radiating sunburst/rays behind hero panels, overlaid with small Ben-Day halftone dots; blue "text placeholder" panels use short horizontal line-bars instead of real text to suggest dense copy |
| Shapes and outlines | Every panel, badge, speech bubble, and character has a thick, uniform black outline with rounded corners; comparison bars/columns are flat-filled rectangles with the same outline treatment |
| Palette accents | Bright cyan starburst price badge, red jagged comic "impact" bursts behind headline numbers, yellow badge with a red circular "call-out" sticker |
| Typography | Bold, chunky, rounded display lettering (comic-lettering style) in black with a white/cream outline halo and drop shadow, used for headlines and big stat callouts; smaller captions sit in yellow strip banners with black text |
| Mascot | A single small orange/terracotta asterisk-flower-shaped blob character wearing a black domino mask and a short red cape, used as a recurring "spokes-character" that reacts, speaks via word balloons, and physically stands on bar-chart columns |
| Composition | Cover = single portrait panel with a red masthead strip (issue number/date box + logo) over a full-bleed sunburst; interior pages = symmetric two-page spread, each page split into a 2x2 grid of rounded-rect panels separated by a visible book gutter/spine with page-thickness shading |

## Direction
Design a comic-book / pop-art motion piece: present the content as a physical vintage comic magazine — a cover, then several two-page 2x2-panel spreads, then a colophon back cover — rendered as a portrait page centered in a landscape frame with a plain dark vignette backdrop. Build each panel from flat, saturated primary/secondary color blocks (red, gold-orange, blue, occasional deep maroon), radiating sunburst ray backgrounds, and Ben-Day halftone dot textures. Outline every shape, panel border, badge, and character uniformly in thick black with rounded corners. Use a single small recurring mascot character with a simple, friendly, rounded silhouette (mask/cape optional) that reacts to on-panel data via word balloons and physically interacts with chart elements (e.g., standing on a bar). Render headline numbers and key stats in chunky, rounded, black-outlined comic-lettering type with drop shadows, punctuated by jagged comic "impact" burst shapes and badge stickers for emphasis. Animate primarily through a slow virtual camera (push-ins and pans across a mostly-static page) rather than character animation, revealing one panel's information at a time with quick pop/scale-in beats for each new element (numbers, strikethroughs, bursts, speech bubbles). Transition between spreads with a paper page-turn/curl effect showing page thickness and a shadowed gutter/spine. Score it with a single continuous, bright, driving instrumental track at a brisk up-tempo pace, mixed hot and consistently loud with frequent percussive transient hits, and align at least one musical riser/impact hit to the video's biggest on-screen reveal. Keep the overall tone punchy, declarative, and optimistic, like a vintage superhero-comic advertisement.
