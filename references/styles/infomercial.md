# Style: home-shopping infomercial

The video reads as a parody of a Korean late-night TV home-shopping / infomercial broadcast: a flat, rounded-vector cartoon world where each beat of the pitch gets its own full-bleed, single-hue "sunburst" backdrop, a chunky comic-shout headline slams onto the screen, and a persistent lower-third news-ticker bar keeps scrolling bullet claims underneath. A simple blob-shaped mascot character stands in for "the customer," shown gloomy and gray before the pitch and pumped-up and colorful after it. There is no camera movement at all — it behaves like a slide deck of hard graphic cards, each card populated with big bold numbers, price tags, comparison panels, and cartoon icons (a ticking bomb, a ringing phone, a rocket, a green checkmark). The audio is a bright, dense, evenly-pulsing music-and-SFX bed that stays "hot" almost the entire runtime, occasionally spiking with comedic riser/whoosh effects timed to the hard cuts, giving the whole thing a caffeinated, game-show-auction energy.

## Signature

| Item | On screen |
|---|---|
| Background treatment | Full-bleed radial "sunburst" rays fill every scene in one saturated hue; the hue itself is the scene-to-scene variable (measured: near-black charcoal `#131313`/`#1F1F1F` for a "gloomy before" beat, golden `#FED84A`/`#FEB812` for a product-reveal beat, alert red `#D80011`/`#DC0111` for a price-drop beat, electric blue `#1C38FF`/`#385BFD` for a comparison/CTA beat, purple-to-magenta `#851C92`/`#5B088A` for a phone-in-now beat, and a dark warm-brown vignette `#130D08`→`#592917` for the closing card) |
| Palette accents | Ticker tag red `#E70010`/`#E80012`, ticker scroll-bar yellow `#FED300`, stat/progress-bar orange `#FE7A19`, confirmation-badge green `#18B44B`, neutral flat gray `#DDDDDD` for the "before" state and inactive bars, white card fills with heavy black strokes for comparison panels |
| Shapes / icons | Flat, thick-outlined cartoon props: a bomb with a lit, sparking fuse; a corded phone receiver mid-ring with motion arcs; a cardboard-box product reveal rendered in simple isometric-style panels; a rocket; a circular green checkmark badge; small 4-point sparkle/twinkle accents scattered over every backdrop |
| Typography / type feel | Oversized, ultra-bold "comic shout" display lettering (rounded slab caps) with heavy black outline and a offset drop shadow, used for headlines in both Latin caps and the local script; a bold rounded sans for body callouts and big price numbers; a plain thin white sans reserved for small print / source-citation footnotes bottom-left |
| Composition | Fixed top-left "LIVE" red pill badge plus a small channel/show-name tag, present in every frame; a permanent lower-third ticker strip (red label chip + yellow scrolling-text bar) running the entire video; comparison beats use two-column or two-row bordered white cards (old value struck through in red, replaced by a bigger new value) with a jagged starburst "percent-off" badge pinned to the corner |
| Transitions | Hard cuts only — no dissolves, wipes, or camera moves; each cut is preceded by a 1–2 frame "flash" card (a near-blank pastel field with a small white impact starburst and a short caption) that functions like a whip-flash/impact sting bridging one saturated-hue scene to the next |
| Signature flourish | A cartoon bomb icon with a burning fuse used as a recurring "big number incoming" beat, visually priming a hard cut into the next price/stat card — read as the style's connective tissue between claims |

## Direction
Render a fast-cut, static-camera "shopping-channel infomercial parody" in flat, rounded 2D vector illustration. Every beat of the pitch is its own full-bleed scene filled with saturated radial "sunburst" rays in a single bold hue (cycle through moody near-black, hype gold, alert red, confident electric blue, urgent magenta/purple, and a warm dark vignette for the close) — change the hue scene-to-scene rather than blending it. Keep a persistent top-left "LIVE" badge and small show-name tag in every frame, plus a permanent lower-third ticker bar (a colored label chip feeding into a bright scrolling text bar) that runs the whole piece. Use oversized ultra-bold "comic shout" display lettering with heavy black outlines and offset drop shadows for headlines, mixed with a rounded sans for supporting numbers and callouts, and reserve a thin plain sans for small-print attribution text tucked in a corner. Populate scenes with flat, thick-outlined cartoon props appropriate to the new subject (a simple blob-style mascot character shown gloomy/gray in a "before" state and colorful/triumphant in an "after" state; comparison beats as bordered white cards with an old value struck through in red and replaced by a bigger new value, pinned with a jagged starburst badge; at least one "big reveal" icon rendered like a product box or package; a call-to-action icon such as a ringing phone; a confirmation checkmark badge). Cut hard between scenes — no dissolves or camera movement — bridging each cut with a one-or-two-frame flash card (a near-blank pastel field with a small white impact burst) so scenes feel detonated into one another rather than eased. Score it with a bright, dense, evenly-pulsing rhythmic music bed that comes in hot within the first second or two and stays energetic and loud almost throughout, accented with short percussive transient hits and the occasional comedic riser/whoosh timed to the hard cuts; keep one clearly quieter cold-open moment before the energy locks in, and let the single loudest musical/SFX hit land on the most urgent, final call-to-action beat.
