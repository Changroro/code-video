# Style: assembly manual

The video reads like a printed IKEA-style product-assembly instruction booklet that has been turned into a motion explainer: flat vector line art on aged cream paper, a giant chapter numeral opening every page, an isometric shipping-box "hero" object, a small stick-figure mascot for scale, and literal hardware metaphors (price tags, coin stacks, a conveyor belt, wall outlets) standing in for abstract data instead of ordinary charts. Pages slide over one another like leaves of a manual being turned rather than cutting, each diagram builds itself piece by piece before its number snaps in, and a steady rhythmic tick-track under occasional synth swells gives the whole thing a calm, deliberate, "assembled and verified" feeling that ends on a stamped checkmark held in a still frame.

## Signature
| Item | On screen |
|---|---|
| Background | Warm cream paper, solid core `#F0EEE4` with a soft vignette toward the edges (`#E1DDD1`); flat, no gradients, same tone on every single page |
| Line and ink | Uniform bold warm-black outlines (`#191A18`) on every shape; clean flat vector, no soft shadows or gradients except a thin drop-shadow where one page slides over another |
| Palette accents | Neutral tan/putty fill for the "old/base" object (`#E4E0D1` lit face, `#CCC6B6` shaded face), a single blush-salmon fill for the "new/better" object (`#F4D7CA`), and one terracotta/burnt-orange (`#D0643A`) reserved only for hero stats, badges, checkmarks and deltas |
| Typography | Heavy grotesque bold sans headline (Latin + a matching bold Korean gothic), an oversized numeral leading each chapter like a manual step, a lighter warm-gray subtitle line beneath it, tiny warm-gray footer citation and page number in the bottom corners of every screen |
| Iconography as metaphor | Abstract stats are shown as literal objects: a price tag for cost, a coin stack for spend, a conveyor belt feeding out of a machine chute for throughput, rows of a small repeated fence/arch icon as an isotype pictogram grid for a ratio, wall electrical outlets for platform availability, an isometric shipping box for the product, a plain stick figure for human scale |
| Comparison layout | Nearly every page is a strict two-column "before vs after": left = old/neutral tan, right = new/salmon, linked by a thin arrow, a dividing line, or a shared baseline |
| Change device | A decrease is never just deleted — the old value gets a diagonal cross-out (X) and the removed portion is left behind as a pale dashed "ghost" outline (dashed coins, dashed pictogram icons, a struck-through price tag) |
| Hero-stat badge | The headline number or a completion checkmark sits inside a bold circular ring stamp in the accent orange, like a rubber "approved" stamp on the manual's closing page |

## Direction
Design a short explainer video as a flat-vector "assembly manual" infographic: a warm cream-paper background with a subtle edge vignette held constant across every screen, bold warm-black outlines on every shape, and no gradients or soft shadows except a thin drop-shadow where one page slides over the next. Open each section with an oversized numeral acting as a manual step, paired with a bold sans headline and a smaller gray subtitle line, plus a tiny source-citation line and page number in the bottom corners of every screen, like a printed instruction booklet. Represent every abstract stat with a literal physical object or metaphor — tags, stacked coins, a conveyor belt, wall outlets, a shipping box, an isotype pictogram grid of one small repeated icon — instead of plain charts. Keep a strict two-column "before vs after" or "old vs new" comparison on nearly every screen: neutral tan/putty for the baseline side, a single blush-salmon fill for the improved side, and reserve one terracotta/burnt-orange accent exclusively for hero numbers, percentage badges, checkmarks and deltas. Show any decrease by crossing out the old value and leaving a dashed ghost outline of what was removed, never a plain deletion. Animate each screen by building its diagram first (bars, stacks or icon rows growing or populating in), then snapping in the number or label, then optionally stamping a circular orange badge or checkmark for the headline takeaway. Move between sections with a fast horizontal slide/page-wipe — the next page covers the previous one with a shadow at the moving seam — rather than a dissolve or hard cut; open directly on the title with no fade-in and close by holding the final frame still instead of fading to black. Add a small stick-figure mascot somewhere for scale or approval. Score it with a steady, fast, unobtrusive tick/pulse bed that gets noticeably denser under the busiest visual reveals, add a synth swell under the single biggest stat of the piece, and finish with a short descending glitch/sweep tail into the closing stamp before a quick fade to silence.
